# VS-39 — Operational Signal Recommendations Design

## Outcome

Turn the normalized, PII-free Signals and Changes delivered by VS-38 into evidence-backed Today recommendations. VS-39 closes the usefulness gap between collecting operational data and recommending a practical next action. It does not change connector access, ingestion, raw-file handling, navigation, or production infrastructure.

## Product boundary

VS-39 implements a hybrid intelligence model:

- provider-neutral operational rules recognize trustworthy metric movements across supported Businesses;
- Restaurant & Café Knowledge Pack patterns translate relevant movements into category-appropriate actions;
- all category-specific thresholds, eligibility rules, copy, goal mappings, and execution templates remain in the Knowledge Pack boundary;
- the connector, CSV normalizer, operational projector, bundle resolver, and generic generator remain provider-neutral.

Initial supported metrics are `gross-sales`, `orders`, `repeat-orders`, and `delivery-time`. Unknown metrics remain stored and attributable but do not create recommendations until an approved Knowledge Pack supports them.

## Evidence contract

Recommendations use derived `BusinessChange` evidence rather than inferring trends from isolated observations. Eligible evidence must:

1. project through the operational evidence layer with exact change and underlying Signal IDs;
2. use a complete deterministic 7-day or 28-day comparison generated by VS-38;
3. be `fresh` or explicitly `stale`, never `historical`;
4. have non-zero comparison context where a percentage threshold is required;
5. match the metric, direction, window, and materiality required by the selected pattern;
6. remain Business-isolated and goal-aligned through the existing Opportunity generator.

Fresh evidence is preferred over stale evidence. A stale fact can support a low-confidence candidate only when the Knowledge Pack pattern explicitly permits it. Historical, malformed, unsupported, or immaterial changes create no candidate.

## Deterministic materiality

The initial Restaurant & Café pack supports these bounded observations:

- gross sales decline of at least 10% over a complete 7-day or 28-day comparison;
- order decline of at least 10% over a complete 7-day or 28-day comparison;
- repeat-order decline of at least 10% over a complete 7-day or 28-day comparison;
- delivery-time deterioration of at least 10% over a complete 7-day or 28-day comparison.

Positive changes and changes inside the threshold remain attributable evidence but do not generate a corrective recommendation in this slice. Threshold equality is eligible. Percentage evaluation uses the persisted decimal values, not rounded display text. A comparison value of zero does not qualify for a percentage-based recommendation.

These thresholds are pilot policy encoded in the packaged manifest, not universal causal claims. Future threshold changes require a new exact Knowledge Pack version and normal certification.

## Knowledge Pack extensions

The manifest contract gains explicit operational evidence requirements rather than embedding metric parsing in a switch statement. Each operational requirement declares:

- metric key;
- accepted direction;
- minimum absolute relative change;
- accepted windows;
- accepted freshness levels;
- minimum evidence count.

Restaurant patterns reference those requirements using the existing evidence-rule model. Initial patterns are:

- `sales-decline-review` for revenue/growth goals;
- `order-decline-review` for revenue/growth goals;
- `repeat-order-decline-review` for retention/customer-experience goals;
- `delivery-time-deterioration-review` for efficiency/customer-experience goals.

The generic Core pack does not duplicate these Restaurant actions. Generic Businesses can carry operational facts through the provider-neutral bundle, but unsupported facts produce no filler recommendation.

## Recommendation semantics

Every generated candidate:

- says Atlas **observed** a movement over named comparison periods;
- describes a bounded review or experiment the owner controls;
- never says the movement was caused by a channel, menu item, employee, promotion, customer segment, or external event;
- retains exact operational evidence IDs in the Opportunity snapshot;
- preserves assumptions and limitations, including that correlation is not causation and results are not guaranteed;
- uses the existing goal priority, category-specific preference, confidence ordering, cooldown identity, and no-filler selection policy.

Operational evidence may change a candidate's cooldown fingerprint because the exact evidence IDs change. Unrelated evidence remains unable to bypass cooldown.

## Candidate examples

For a fresh 7-day gross-sales decline aligned to a revenue goal, Atlas may recommend reviewing the weakest trading periods and preparing one owner-approved demand experiment. It must not invent a weak weekday unless the operational data contains that dimension.

For delivery-time deterioration aligned to an efficiency goal, Atlas may recommend reviewing the ordering-to-handover workflow and preparing a bounded operational checklist. It must not blame staff, couriers, providers, or demand volume.

## Error and degraded behavior

- Unsupported metric: retain evidence; generate no candidate.
- Historical evidence: exclude from candidate resolution.
- Stale evidence: allow only where explicitly configured and reduce confidence to Low.
- Missing/zero comparison for percentage rule: generate no candidate.
- Malformed provenance or window: generate no candidate and preserve existing safe degradation.
- Multiple eligible operational patterns: use existing deterministic ranking; never emit random or filler ordering.
- Connector unavailable after prior sync: previously normalized eligible evidence keeps its recorded freshness behavior; the generator does not pretend a current sync succeeded.

## Data and API impact

No migration or new endpoint is required. VS-39 consumes the VS-38 entities, operational projection, resolved bundle, and Opportunity persistence. Manifest schema/fingerprint changes are additive packaged-code changes and require exact-version updates where mandated by the existing manifest policy.

The Today and Opportunity Detail APIs keep their current shapes. Operational evidence appears through the existing evidence snapshot fields. Mobile screens keep the approved visual hierarchy and render existing title, reason, why-now, confidence, evidence, assumptions, limitations, and execution-kit content without a new route or tab.

## Security and privacy

- No raw CSV, customer PII, customer-level segmentation, payment secret, Drive token, or connector credential enters a recommendation.
- No external write action or provider-specific API is introduced.
- Evidence remains owner-authorized and Business-isolated.
- Recommendation content is deterministic packaged policy; no AI provider or prompt is added.
- No production release, production deployment, EAS build/submit/OTA, or production database mutation is authorized.

## Testing

Strict RED/GREEN coverage must prove:

- each supported metric, direction, threshold equality, below-threshold exclusion, and accepted window;
- positive/unchanged/zero-comparison/unsupported/historical/malformed evidence exclusion;
- stale evidence confidence degradation;
- exact change and underlying Signal provenance in the candidate snapshot;
- goal matching and category isolation;
- deterministic ranking when multiple operational patterns qualify;
- same-evidence cooldown suppression and changed-relevant-evidence reconsideration;
- unchanged existing Knowledge Pack, Opportunity, Today, mobile, migration, governance, security, and product-intake gates.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Fresh material operational declines can produce a Restaurant-specific, goal-aligned Today candidate.
2. Unsupported or weak evidence produces no filler candidate.
3. Every candidate is observational, non-causal, attributable, deterministic, and owner-controlled.
4. Existing ranking and cooldown policies remain authoritative.
5. No connector, persistence, API-shape, navigation, privacy, deployment, or production boundary is expanded.
6. Exact-head CI, Security baseline, Product Intake, and PES certification pass before merge.
