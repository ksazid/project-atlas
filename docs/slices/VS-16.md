# VS-16 — URL-First Business Discovery & Owner Confirmation

## Purpose

Deliver the first bounded Category Intelligence Foundation slice: authenticated owners can paste an ordinary public HTTPS business website, Wolt page or Bolt Food page; Atlas conservatively discovers supported public facts; the owner reviews/edits/confirms them; Atlas creates the initial Business while retaining provenance. Manual setup remains available.

## Requirements

- FR-02 — Business creation
- FR-03 — Business Profile
- FR-05 — Business Context / public data minimisation and confirmation
- Locked Category Intelligence Foundation direction
- ATLAS-DESIGN-001 v1.2

## Acceptance criteria

1. URL-first setup is the primary Business setup path; manual setup is an explicit fallback.
2. Ordinary HTTPS business websites plus Wolt/Bolt public pages are supported through a provider-neutral Public Business Snapshot.
3. Unsafe/private/local URL targets and automatic redirects are blocked.
4. Discovery extracts only directly supported facts; absent values remain unknown.
5. Every public observation retains provider/source URL/observed timestamp/confidence/evidence class.
6. Public observations are not authoritative until the owner confirms them.
7. Owner-edited values become owner-reported authoritative facts while the original observation remains provenance evidence.
8. Canonical category/subcategory values use the Atlas taxonomy; Generic Business is the fallback.
9. Initial Business creation from discovery is account-isolated, one-time and atomic with membership, Generic Business Knowledge Pack assignment, profile/provenance and audit.
10. Existing manual `POST /api/v1/businesses` remains compatible.
11. Welcome/setup use the approved Atlas tokens and BrandMark boundary; no screen-level Starbucks or fabricated demo business facts remain.
12. Loading, invalid/unsafe URL, source unavailable, partial discovery, retry, manual fallback, validation, create failure/draft preservation and success states are covered.
13. Mobile accessibility includes semantic labels, busy/error state, non-colour provenance/confidence, keyboard-safe layout, reduced-motion-safe behavior and >=44pt targets.
14. CI includes policy/model/runtime tests, clean PostgreSQL migrations, .NET tests/build, Security baseline and Product Intake at the exact implementation SHA.
15. Release and production remain unauthorized.

## Out of scope

- private Wolt/Bolt/POS/reservation/accounting connectors;
- authenticated scraping or browser automation;
- Knowledge Pack Schema v2;
- category-specific progressive questions;
- Restaurant/Cafe opportunity recipes/metric engine;
- inferred sales, profitability, demographics, repeat rate, utilisation or popularity;
- release, deployment or production enablement.

## Security boundary

Generic website fetching is an SSRF boundary. HTTPS only, no URL credentials, no localhost/private/reserved targets, connection-time public-IP validation, no automatic redirects, bounded HTML size, short timeout and HTML-compatible content only.
