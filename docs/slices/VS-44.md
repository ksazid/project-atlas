# VS-44 — Connectors Hub & Live Signal Status

## Outcome
Profile routes Business Data into a dedicated Connectors hub inspired by the approved reference while retaining Atlas's established visual language. Business Context and Business Data use the same card architecture.

## Product rules
- Google Drive remains the primary pilot connector.
- A saved Drive link is **Connected**, not **Live**.
- **Live** requires `lastSuccessfulSyncAt` from a completed sync.
- Google Drive opens the existing Business Data detail where folder connection, sync schedule, sync-now and CSV fallback remain functional.
- CSV upload is presented as Manual Data, not falsely represented as a persistent connector.
- Bolt Food, Wolt, Meta Ads and Instagram may be shown only as non-interactive Coming soon entries until real contracts exist.
- Do not show unrelated integrations merely to populate the catalogue.

## Profile alignment
Business Context and Business Data share surface, border, radius, padding, eyebrow, title, body and text-action treatment. Their content/state differs; their component hierarchy does not.

## Acceptance
1. Profile Business Data CTA is `Manage connectors` and routes to `/connectors`.
2. Connectors hub exposes search and restaurant-relevant connector entries.
3. Drive status distinguishes Connect / Connected / Live.
4. Live is derived only from a recorded successful sync timestamp.
5. Drive row routes to existing `/business-data` detail.
6. Manual CSV route remains accessible.
7. Future connectors are visibly unavailable and cannot initiate fake connection flows.
8. Existing VS-38/40/41 connector behavior remains intact.
