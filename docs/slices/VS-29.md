## Implemented scope

### 1. Public business contact and social evidence

`PublicBusinessExtractor` now adds only bounded, provenance-rich observations:

- structured schema.org `email`;
- allowlisted schema.org `sameAs` social URLs for Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, X/Twitter and YouTube;
- normalized/deduplicated HTTPS social links with fragments removed and existing public-URL safety validation retained;
- one unambiguous public `mailto:` fallback only when structured email is absent;
- one unambiguous public `tel:` fallback only when structured phone is absent;
- conflicting fallback candidates produce **no fact** rather than arbitrary first-write-wins.

No contact information is inferred from arbitrary prose.

### 2. One bounded official-site enrichment hop

After user-supplied sources are processed, Atlas may automatically read **at most one** high-confidence official website URL exposed by the successful primary source.

The contribution is accepted only when:

- the URL passes the existing HTTPS/public/SSRF-safe discovery boundary;
- it was not already supplied by the owner;
- normalized Business name matches exactly; and
- at least one second strong signal matches (`phone` or `primaryLocation`).

An accepted site remains its own secondary `BusinessSourceObservation`, preserving source URL, order, provider, observed time, confidence, media/offering provenance and evidence lineage. A mismatch, timeout, unavailable page, blocked redirect or invalid content cannot invalidate the usable anchor source. Cancellation still propagates.

### 3. Truthful Business Profile materialisation

The existing discovery-confirmation contract now carries `Email`, `SocialChannels` and explicitly confirmed `OpeningHours` without a new schema or migration.

- matching retained public email/social values materialise as `public-observed` + owner-confirmed provenance;
- owner edits that differ from retained evidence remain `owner-reported`;
- transient Place-enrichment hours materialise only after explicit owner confirmation and are stored as owner-reported canonical Profile hours;
- confirmed hours are trimmed, deduplicated, capped at seven entries and bounded by the existing value-size limit;
- hours remain Business Profile data and are not duplicated into Business Context.

Existing owner-confirmed values remain authoritative.

### 4. Semantic progressive-question suppression

The catalogue now recognizes that owner-confirmed, non-empty `operatingchannels` already satisfies equivalent `primarychannels` prompts.

This suppresses both the restaurant service-channel question and equivalent generic primary-channel fallback without suppressing unrelated questions such as busy periods, constraints, customer groups or priorities. Unconfirmed or blank operating-channel data does not suppress anything.

### 5. Mobile discovery/review wiring

The existing `create-business` route remains the only setup/review flow.

- discovered email/social/opening-hours values prefill the existing draft;
- the create request trims and carries email/social;
- explicit About confirmation carries normalized opening hours;
- the edit/review section exposes accessible `Business email` and `Social channels` fields alongside the existing Website, Phone and Opening hours fields;
- the confirmation summary renders only non-empty presence facts;
- the established provider-neutral copy `Observed from public business page` is preserved;
- no new route, tab, navigation restructuring or second form flow was introduced.
