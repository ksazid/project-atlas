# VS-42 — Operational Signal Prioritisation

## Objective

Make Atlas Today’s Focus respond to meaningful fresh operational evidence before generic review opportunities when both serve the same owner-priority goal.

## Problem

Operational ingestion and evidence matching already produce grounded Restaurant & Café opportunities for material movements such as gross-sales decline, order decline, repeat-order decline, and delivery-time deterioration. Candidate selection, however, previously ranked goal priority, confidence, effort, category specificity, and pattern key without distinguishing fresh operational evidence from generic context evidence. A generic pattern could therefore become Today’s Focus even when a material fresh movement was available for the same goal.

## Scope

- Preserve owner goal priority as the first ordering boundary.
- Within the same goal priority, promote candidates backed by valid **fresh** operational-change evidence ahead of candidates without operational evidence.
- Among otherwise eligible fresh operational candidates, prefer the larger absolute observed relative movement before the existing confidence/effort/category/pattern tie-breakers.
- Do not promote stale operational evidence ahead of supported generic evidence.
- Preserve all existing evidence thresholds, supported metrics/windows, category rules, cooldowns, confidence degradation, causation guardrails, and no-filler behavior.

## Explicitly out of scope

- New connector or ingestion behavior.
- New operational metrics or threshold changes.
- Database/schema or migration changes.
- Knowledge-pack content expansion.
- Mobile UI changes.
- Release or production enablement.
- Claims that an observed movement explains why performance changed.

## Acceptance evidence

`tests/api/OperationalSignalPrioritisationVs42Tests.cs` proves:

1. A fresh material gross-sales decline outranks a generic ordering-path review for the same priority revenue goal.
2. When multiple fresh operational movements qualify at the same goal priority, the larger absolute movement is selected first.
3. Stale operational evidence does not displace a supported generic review.

Existing operational opportunity tests remain authoritative for eligibility, thresholds, category isolation, confidence degradation, and evidence provenance.

## Rollback

Revert the VS-42 ranking change. Ingestion, persisted operational evidence, eligibility rules, and existing opportunities remain unchanged.
