# VS-28 — Device-Adaptive Native Polish & Motion

## Outcome

Make the current Atlas mobile experience device-adaptive and native-feeling across modern iOS and Android by replacing fixed page-edge spacing with real safe-area geometry, adding restrained interruptible motion, and using selective iOS 26 Liquid Glass only for floating/structural chrome.

Atlas product behavior, brand identity, information hierarchy, route semantics and domain behavior remain unchanged.

## Material and motion policy

Atlas content surfaces remain the approved warm-neutral/green visual system. Liquid Glass is restricted to floating hierarchy where translucency communicates function. The fallback solid material is a first-class design and must preserve the same geometry, labels and touch targets.

Motion must begin with immediate interaction feedback, remain interruptible, prefer critically damped/no-overshoot springs, use transform/opacity for local feedback where practical, and never delay content or block navigation. Bounce is reserved for genuine momentum-driven gestures.

Reduce Motion removes spatial/elastic motion and uses static changes or restrained fades. Reduce Transparency disables native glass and uses the solid Atlas material.
