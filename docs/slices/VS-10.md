# VS-10 — Weekly Review

## Objective

Provide a concise, owner-facing weekly review derived from authoritative Atlas records so a Business can understand what was recommended, what was acted on, what outcomes were recorded, and what remains open.

## Scope

- business-isolated weekly review API
- deterministic seven-day review window with explicit start/end timestamps
- counts for Opportunities and Action statuses
- completed Action and Outcome summaries
- Execution Kit usage summary
- goal and Knowledge Pack provenance where available
- factual learning summary that distinguishes recorded facts from interpretation
- accessible mobile Weekly Review experience using the existing PES shell
- loading, empty, error and refresh states

## Rules

- no duplicate weekly-review source-of-truth table in VS-10
- derive review from existing authoritative Opportunity, Action Decision, Execution Kit, Outcome and Goal records
- never fabricate missing activity
- never claim causal ROI or guaranteed business impact
- preserve exact Business isolation and owner authorization
- do not mutate History or Business Memory automatically
- PES navigation, routing, authentication, state management and folder conventions remain unchanged
- runtime remains development/test only
- no deployment or production enablement

## Acceptance

1. An authorized Business Owner can retrieve a bounded weekly review for their Business only.
2. The review covers a deterministic seven-day period and returns the period boundaries.
3. Review metrics reconcile to underlying authoritative records.
4. Completed Actions with Outcomes surface their recorded evidence class and result summary.
5. Missing Outcomes are reported as missing, not inferred.
6. Mobile renders the review in the existing PES shell with accessible loading, empty, error and refresh states.
7. CI, Security baseline and Product intake pass before certification.
