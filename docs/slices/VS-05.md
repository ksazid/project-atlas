# VS-05 — Opportunity Detail

## Outcome

Allow a Business owner to inspect the full evidence-backed reasoning behind the current Opportunity without changing the PES Mobile framework or duplicating Today’s Focus logic.

## Requirement

- FR-08 — Opportunity Detail

## Dependencies

- Certified VS-03 Knowledge Pack Foundation
- Certified VS-04 Today’s Focus

## In scope

- dedicated Opportunity Detail experience reached from Today’s Focus;
- goal alignment and the exact Business Goal used;
- Evidence displayed separately from Atlas interpretation;
- Reason and Why Now;
- Confidence and Expected Impact;
- effort, assumptions, expiry and limitations;
- source categories and provenance;
- action summary and route to the future Execution Kit boundary;
- safe expired, unavailable, missing, offline and stale states;
- business isolation, auditability and exact Knowledge Pack version retention;
- API, mobile, tests and synchronized documentation.

## Out of scope

- Execution Kit creation or editing;
- autonomous publishing or external action;
- production AI provider execution;
- embeddings, vector storage or semantic retrieval;
- PES navigation, authentication, state-management or design-system restructuring;
- deployment, release or production enablement.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The owner can open Opportunity Detail from Today’s Focus.
2. Evidence and Atlas interpretation are visually and contractually distinct.
3. Goal alignment, reason, why-now, confidence, impact, effort, assumptions, expiry, source categories and limitations are shown.
4. The detail remains bound to the exact Opportunity, Business and Knowledge Pack version.
5. Cross-business access returns safe not-found behavior.
6. Expired, stale, missing and unavailable states provide a useful next action.
7. The Execution Kit boundary is represented without implementing VS-06.
8. Existing PES Mobile structure remains unchanged.
9. Deterministic validation, API tests, mobile typecheck/lint and migration checks pass.
10. No deployment or production enablement occurs.

## Safety boundary

Runtime remains disabled outside development/test. No release, deployment, production credentials, paid provider activation or production enablement is authorized.
