# VS-04 — Today’s Focus

## Status

Activated for implementation after certified VS-03 was merged into `main`.

## Requirement

Implements **FR-07 — Today’s Focus** from `ATLAS-PRD-001`.

The home experience must present one primary current Opportunity that the owner can understand within ten seconds.

## Product outcome

Give an owner one clear, explainable and actionable next business move without turning Atlas into a dashboard, generic chatbot or autonomous decision-maker.

## In scope

- primary current Opportunity on the existing mobile home experience;
- concise action title;
- why it matters and why now;
- Expected Impact, effort and Confidence;
- evidence summary with facts separated from Atlas interpretation;
- timing or expiry;
- route to the future Execution Kit boundary;
- Apply, Skip and Not Relevant decisions;
- honest no-suitable-opportunity, insufficient-context, expired, offline and unavailable states;
- business isolation, provenance, auditability and deterministic eligibility policy;
- persistence and APIs required for Today’s Focus;
- tests, migration, traceability and synchronized documentation.

## Locked boundaries

- PES Mobile framework structure, navigation, routing, authentication, state management, design system and API-client conventions remain unchanged.
- VS-03 Knowledge Pack persistence and exact-version assignment remain unchanged.
- Atlas may use deterministic development/test candidate fixtures or operator-prepared Opportunities, but no production AI provider is enabled.
- No embeddings, vector database, autonomous publishing, guaranteed outcome claims, deployment, release or production enablement.
- Only one primary Today’s Focus is presented. Atlas must not generate filler when no candidate qualifies.

## Initial domain direction

The slice will introduce an Opportunity aggregate that retains:

- Business and goal alignment;
- exact Knowledge Pack key/version reference;
- action title and explanation;
- evidence and interpretation as separate structures;
- confidence, effort and expected-impact assessments;
- timing, expiry and eligibility status;
- provenance and policy metadata;
- owner decision state and audit history.

The detailed schema remains subject to implementation review, but it must preserve historical exact-version references and support later Opportunity Detail, Execution Kit, feedback, outcome and History slices without redesigning the core aggregate.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The owner sees at most one eligible primary Opportunity on Home.
2. The Opportunity is understandable within ten seconds.
3. Why it matters, why now, impact, effort, confidence, evidence and expiry are visible.
4. Evidence and Atlas interpretation are distinguishable.
5. Apply, Skip and Not Relevant are recorded with safe concurrency and audit history.
6. Expired, duplicated, unsupported, unsafe or materially irrelevant Opportunities are not shown.
7. When no candidate qualifies, Atlas states that honestly and provides the next useful action.
8. Cross-business access is denied without leaking existence.
9. Historical Opportunities retain the exact assigned Knowledge Pack key/version.
10. Mobile accessibility, degraded states and offline behavior are covered.
11. Existing PES and VS-03 architecture remains intact.
12. Runtime remains disabled outside development/test; no deployment or production enablement occurs.
