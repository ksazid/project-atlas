# VS-03 — Knowledge Pack Foundation

## Outcome

Introduce the provider-neutral, immutable and versioned Knowledge Pack foundation required by FR-06.

## Scope

- define the Knowledge Pack contract and validation rules;
- provide the Generic Business Knowledge Pack as the MVP default;
- assign at least one active pack to every Business;
- retain exact pack key and version for downstream Opportunity history;
- expose Business-isolated read APIs for active packs;
- add persistence, migration, tests and traceability;
- keep runtime disabled outside development and test.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A Business always has at least one active Knowledge Pack.
2. The Generic Business Knowledge Pack is available and versioned.
3. Pack content is immutable after publication.
4. Pack key and exact version are stored separately from display metadata.
5. Core business logic remains industry-agnostic.
6. Invalid, duplicate or unsupported pack assignments are rejected safely.
7. Cross-Business access returns a safe not-found response.
8. Migration, policy, isolation and contract tests cover the foundation.

## Exclusions

- Opportunity generation;
- Today’s Focus;
- AI provider execution;
- production deployment;
- production credentials;
- release or production enablement.
