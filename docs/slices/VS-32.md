## Delivered scope

- append-only Business-scoped feedback records for Opportunity ratings, incorrect-context reports, unsafe-guidance reports, general feedback and support requests;
- safe same-Business validation for referenced Opportunities;
- bounded optional owner notes with explicit privacy guidance;
- owner `POST /api/v1/businesses/{businessId}/feedback` endpoint;
- forward-only PostgreSQL migration `20260812113000_FeedbackSupport`;
- Opportunity Detail useful/not-useful rating independent from Action Decision and Outcome lifecycle;
- Opportunity Detail unsafe-guidance reporting deep link;
- Context incorrect-context reporting entry point kept separate from direct Context editing;
- Settings Feedback & Support entry point;
- reusable pushed `/feedback` Stack detail screen with failure draft preservation, duplicate-submit blocking and success/error states;
- VS-31 Today / History / Goals / Profile persistent navigation preserved exactly.

## Safety and authority boundary

- unsafe-guidance reports are append-only review signals; they do not automatically withdraw, suppress, expire or mutate an Opportunity, Action Decision or Outcome;
- incorrect-context reports do not overwrite Business Context;
- cross-Business Opportunity references fail safely without tenant leakage;
- no attachments;
- no external helpdesk/CRM, email/chat transport, queue, Redis dependency or automated moderation;
- owner notes are optional, trimmed and capped at 1200 characters;
- mobile copy warns owners not to include customer names, contact details or other end-customer personal data.
