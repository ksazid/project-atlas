# VS-32 — Feedback, Safety Reporting & Support

## State

Runtime implementation slice activated from fresh post-VS-31 `main@9cd153aae1f0e0962548ecf2cfb5f112350a5a73`.

## Requirements

- FR-17 — Feedback & Support
- FR-16 — Empty and degraded states

## Approved scope

- append-only Business-scoped feedback records for Opportunity ratings, incorrect-context reports, unsafe-guidance reports, general feedback and support requests;
- safe same-Business validation for referenced Opportunities;
- bounded optional owner notes with explicit privacy guidance;
- one owner `POST /api/v1/businesses/{businessId}/feedback` endpoint;
- a forward-only PostgreSQL migration for feedback persistence;
- Opportunity Detail useful/not-useful rating and unsafe-guidance reporting;
- Context incorrect-context reporting entry point;
- Settings Feedback & Support entry point;
- one reusable pushed `/feedback` mobile screen;
- preserve VS-31 Today / History / Goals / Profile persistent navigation exactly.

## Safety and authority boundary

- unsafe-guidance reports are recorded for later authorised review; they do not automatically withdraw, suppress, expire or mutate an Opportunity;
- incorrect-context reports do not overwrite Business Context;
- cross-Business Opportunity references must fail safely without tenant leakage;
- no attachments;
- no external helpdesk/CRM, email/chat transport, queue, Redis dependency or automated moderation;
- the owner note is optional, trimmed and capped at 1200 characters;
- mobile copy tells owners not to include customer names, contact details or other end-customer personal data.

## Out of scope

- FR-18 operator queue, triage, manual intervention and content withdrawal;
- operator profile/context correction;
- feedback edit/delete/history for owners;
- external support-provider integrations;
- analytics dashboards;
- recommendation/ranking changes;
- production deployment, release, EAS build/submit/OTA, production enablement or production database mutation.

## Acceptance boundary

VS-32 passes only when deterministic evidence shows that:

1. every approved feedback kind obeys its kind-specific validation contract;
2. notes are trimmed/bounded and no attachment surface exists;
3. Opportunity references are isolated to the authenticated owner's Business;
4. unsafe reports do not mutate Opportunity state/version;
5. feedback records persist append-only with Business/account/time provenance;
6. the clean PostgreSQL migration chain applies successfully;
7. Opportunity Detail, Context and Settings expose the approved owner entry points;
8. `/feedback` preserves drafts on failure, blocks duplicate in-flight submission, and exposes accessible success/error states;
9. VS-31 four-tab navigation remains unchanged;
10. exact final SHA passes repository CI, Security baseline and Product Intake.

No release, deployment or production enablement is authorized by activating this slice.
