# VS-06 — Execution Kit

## Outcome

Allow a Business owner to turn an applied Opportunity into a reviewable, editable and reusable Execution Kit without autonomous publishing or external action.

## Requirement

- FR-09 — Execution Kit

## Dependencies

- Certified VS-03 Knowledge Pack Foundation
- Certified VS-04 Today’s Focus
- Certified VS-05 Opportunity Detail

## In scope

- create or retrieve one versioned Execution Kit for an eligible Opportunity;
- supported asset types: checklist, review response, business update, social caption, offer wording, message template, campaign brief, operational checklist and measurement suggestion;
- owner review and editing where relevant;
- copy tracking, mark-used state, usefulness rating and Action completion boundary;
- exact Business, Opportunity, Goal and Knowledge Pack provenance;
- deterministic development/test generation using approved templates;
- optimistic concurrency, audit records and Business isolation;
- safe missing, expired, unavailable, stale and unsupported-asset states;
- mobile experience reached from Opportunity Detail;
- API, persistence, migration, tests and synchronized documentation.

## Out of scope

- autonomous publishing, sending or external system execution;
- production AI provider activation;
- image or video generation;
- campaign scheduling, ad buying or financial action;
- outcome attribution and Business Memory learning, which belong to later slices;
- PES navigation, authentication, state-management or design-system restructuring;
- deployment, release or production enablement.

## Acceptance criteria

1. An eligible Opportunity can expose a versioned Execution Kit.
2. Every asset has a supported type, title, editable content where relevant and clear provenance.
3. The owner can edit, copy, mark used and rate usefulness.
4. No endpoint publishes, sends or executes externally.
5. The Kit remains bound to the exact Business, Opportunity and Knowledge Pack version.
6. Cross-Business access returns safe not-found behavior.
7. Stale updates are rejected with a recoverable response.
8. Mobile states cover loading, missing, unavailable and unsupported content.
9. Existing PES Mobile structure remains unchanged.
10. CI, security, migration, API tests and mobile checks pass.

## Safety boundary

Runtime remains disabled outside development/test. No release, deployment, external publishing, production credentials, paid provider activation or production enablement is authorized.
