# VS-02 — Business Profile and Context

## Outcome

Enable an authenticated Business owner to review and maintain a confirmed Business Profile, select and prioritise Business Goals, and establish the minimum context Atlas needs to remain useful without forcing full setup.

## Requirements

- FR-03 — Business Profile
- FR-04 — Business Goals
- FR-05 — Business Context

## Scope

### Business Profile

- Review and update Business identity, category, description, address, locations, operating hours, website, social channels, business channels, preferred language, timezone, currency and owner preferences.
- Distinguish owner-provided values from publicly sourced values.
- Require owner confirmation before publicly sourced values become trusted context.
- Preserve Business-level isolation and optimistic concurrency.

### Business Goals

- Select generic goals covering revenue, profitability, acquisition, retention, reputation, reduced waste, saved time and operational consistency.
- Add custom goals.
- Prioritise and update active goals.
- Prevent empty or duplicate goal definitions.

### Business Context

- Build a minimum useful context projection from confirmed profile, active goals, prior decisions, feedback, outcomes, enabled sources and active Knowledge Packs.
- Keep Atlas useful when context is incomplete.
- Expose clear incomplete, stale, unconfirmed, unavailable and offline-safe states.
- Minimise collected data and avoid end-customer personal data.

## Acceptance criteria

1. An authenticated owner can retrieve only the Business Profile for a Business they own.
2. An owner can update supported profile fields with validation and optimistic concurrency protection.
3. Publicly sourced values are labelled with provenance and cannot be treated as confirmed until the owner approves them.
4. An owner can select, create, prioritise, update and deactivate Business Goals.
5. Duplicate active goals and invalid priorities are rejected safely.
6. Atlas can produce a minimum Business Context projection from limited confirmed data.
7. Missing context does not block the product; the API and mobile experience explain the next useful action.
8. Cross-Business reads and writes return safe not-found or forbidden responses without leaking existence.
9. Profile, goal and confirmation changes are auditable.
10. Mobile screens support loading, empty, validation, stale-version, offline and recoverable error states.
11. Automated tests cover isolation, provenance confirmation, goal prioritisation and degraded-context behaviour.
12. No deployment, production credentials, external publishing or autonomous action is introduced.

## Non-goals

- Today’s Focus generation
- Opportunity scoring or recommendations
- Knowledge Pack implementation beyond retaining active pack references
- External data connectors
- Autonomous publishing
- Production deployment

## Risk and security

Risk level: high.

The slice extends the Business data boundary and introduces owner-confirmed context. All endpoints must remain authenticated, Business-isolated, auditable and privacy-minimised.

## Certification evidence

Expected evidence includes migration validation, API and domain tests, Business-isolation tests, mobile validation/accessibility coverage, traceability and exact-head workflow results.
