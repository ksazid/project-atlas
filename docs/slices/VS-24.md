# VS-24 — End-to-End Category Intelligence Validation

## Status

Active dependency branch — independent mobile-state implementation may proceed; integrated certification is blocked until VS-22 and VS-23 are merged into `main`.

## Requirements

- FR-02 Business creation
- FR-03 Business Profile
- FR-04 Business Goals
- FR-05 Business Context

- FR-07 Today’s Focus

## Goal

Validate the Restaurant/Café Category Intelligence hero journey from public discovery through owner confirmation, Context/Goals, exact Knowledge Bundle resolution, evidence-aware Today’s Focus, Opportunity Detail and Execution Kit, including the real runtime regressions found during isolated device testing.

## In scope

- mobile Today support for `ready`, `insufficient-context`, `no-focus`, `degraded`;
- deterministic cross-slice Restaurant/Café journey tests after VS-22 integration;
- bounded fixes for integration defects only;
- regression matrix for observed discovery/location/confirmation/Today/Detail defects;
- isolated public-Bolt smoke after deterministic gates pass;
- exact-head certification evidence.

## Out of scope

- new provider categories/connectors;
- new AI provider/model work;
- new opportunity recipes;
- redesign of approved screens;
- production deployment.

## Dependency rule

This branch currently contains the VS-23 pre-integration work and does not yet contain VS-22. It must not be certified or merged until VS-22 and VS-23 have landed on `main`, this branch is reconciled to that final baseline, and all deterministic + live isolated validation is rerun.
