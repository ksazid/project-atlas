# VS-01 — Identity and Business Setup

Status: Approved for specification and activation

## Goal
Establish the secure Atlas account and Business foundation so an owner can authenticate, create the first Business and enter the minimum trusted context required for later intelligence slices.

## Requirement IDs
- FR-01 — Identity and access
- FR-02 — Business creation

## User-visible outcome
A first-time owner can create an account, sign in securely, create a Business and reach an authenticated Atlas shell bound to that Business. Returning owners can restore a valid session, sign out safely and recover access through the configured identity provider.

## In scope
- Mobile authorization-code flow with PKCE through a provider-neutral identity adapter.
- Secure session establishment, refresh, logout and local credential cleanup.
- Account lifecycle foundations for recovery, export and deletion requests.
- Initial Business creation with name, category, country, timezone, currency, primary location and operating status.
- One primary BusinessOwner membership created atomically with the Business.
- Restricted internal role foundations for PilotOperator and PlatformAdministrator.
- ASP.NET Core API bootstrap, OpenAPI, health endpoints and problem-details-compatible errors.
- PostgreSQL persistence, EF Core migrations and optimistic concurrency foundations.
- Expo secure storage for tokens and sensitive session material.
- Mobile loading, validation, error, retry and offline-safe states.
- Business isolation, authorization, audit and telemetry coverage.

## Non-goals
- Business Profile enrichment beyond the minimum creation fields.
- Goals, Context Facts, Knowledge Packs or Today’s Focus.
- Social login provider selection as a product commitment.
- Multi-owner collaboration or customer-managed role administration.
- Full pilot operations UI.
- Production deployment, production credentials or release authorization.

## Dependencies
- Approved Atlas PRD v1.0 and TRD v1.0.
- Identity provider decision remains adapter-bound and configuration-driven.
- PostgreSQL development/test environment.

## UI/API/data impact
- Mobile routes: authentication, onboarding, Business creation and authenticated shell.
- API modules: Identity and Businesses.
- Initial contracts for session/account state and Business creation.
- Initial database schemas/tables for accounts, provider mappings, Businesses, memberships and audit records.

## Security impact
High-risk slice because it introduces authentication, authorization, Business isolation, secure token handling and sensitive account data. No client secret may ship in the mobile application. Server authorization must derive identity and Business membership from validated claims and server-side records.

## Observability
- Correlation ID on authentication and Business commands.
- Stable error codes without credential, token or sensitive-data leakage.
- Metrics for sign-in outcome, session restoration, Business creation outcome and authorization denial.
- Audit records for account creation, Business creation, role assignment and logout/revocation events where supported.

## Tests
- Domain and application policy tests for account and Business creation invariants.
- API authorization and Business-isolation tests.
- PostgreSQL migration and integration tests.
- Mobile validation, loading, error, retry and accessibility tests.
- Secure-storage and logout-clearing tests.
- Contract and architecture-boundary tests.

## Acceptance criteria
1. A new owner can authenticate using Authorization Code + PKCE without a mobile client secret.
2. A valid authenticated owner can create exactly one initial Business with the required minimum fields.
3. Business creation and primary BusinessOwner membership are atomic and idempotency-safe.
4. An owner cannot read or mutate another Business by supplying a different Business identifier.
5. Internal roles cannot be self-assigned by a customer.
6. Logout clears secure local session material and invalidates or revokes server session state where supported.
7. Authentication and Business errors use stable, customer-safe problem details.
8. Database migrations are forward-only and validated in CI.
9. Mobile screens support accessible labels, dynamic text, reduced motion and non-colour error communication.
10. CI, Security baseline, Product intake and slice validation pass at the exact head SHA before implementation approval or merge.
