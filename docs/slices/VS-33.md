# VS-33 — Today Experience Simplification

## Status

Active. Product design approved in chat. Runtime implementation is authorized, but must follow the approved Superpowers spec/plan and TDD before production code changes.

## Requirements

- FR-07 — Today’s Focus
- FR-10 — Action decisions
- FR-16 — Empty and degraded states

## Goal

Make Today feel like a calm daily assistant: concise, refreshable, easy to scan, and easy to act on within one or two taps, while preserving one primary evidence-qualified Opportunity.

## Approved scope

- rename the heavy in-page recommendation presentation to **Best move**;
- concise Today header and successful freshness cue;
- native pull-to-refresh as a first-class interaction;
- compact Impact / Effort / Confidence indicators;
- quick actions: Apply, Why?, Later, Not relevant;
- Later maps to the existing Skip decision semantics and does not create a new backend state;
- progressive disclosure: Why Now, Evidence, assumptions, limitations, expiry and Knowledge Pack metadata remain in Opportunity Detail rather than competing on Today;
- concise loading, insufficient-context, no-focus and degraded states;
- restrained semantic supporting tints under the locked Atlas visual language;
- preserve accessibility, Dynamic Type, safe owner-control semantics and the four persistent tabs Today / History / Goals / Profile.

## Runtime boundary

Allowed runtime paths:

- `apps/mobile/src/features/today-focus/**`
- `tests/mobile/**`

Governance/documentation paths:

- `delivery/**`
- `docs/**`

Avoid `AtlasScreen`, Expo Router shell, API, database, migrations, infrastructure and release workflows unless a verified blocker proves the approved design cannot be implemented without them.

## Explicit non-goals

- Business Pulse;
- What Changed analytics;
- connectors;
- revenue/order/menu/channel/competitor/profitability metrics;
- anomaly detection;
- forecasts;
- Ask Atlas;
- Opportunity queue;
- new Action Decision states;
- API/database changes;
- production release or deployment.

## Product and safety rules

- Today remains a decision screen, not a dashboard.
- One primary Best move and one primary Apply action.
- No filler recommendation and no weakening of VS-30 factual-evidence eligibility.
- No automatic owner decisions or goal mutation.
- No fabricated business or BI data.
- Refresh failures must not claim stale data is current.
- Server-confirmed mutation semantics remain authoritative.
- Industry assumptions must not leak into Atlas Core.
