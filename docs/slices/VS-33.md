## Requirements

- FR-07 — Today’s Focus
- FR-10 — Action decisions
- FR-16 — Empty and degraded states

## Goal

Make Today feel like a calm daily assistant: concise, refreshable, easy to scan, and easy to act on within one or two taps, while preserving one primary evidence-qualified Opportunity.

## Delivered scope

- replaced the heavy in-page recommendation presentation with **Best move**;
- concise `Today` heading, one-line orientation and successful freshness cue (`Updated just now`);
- native pull-to-refresh on Ready and safe non-ready Today states;
- manual refresh preserves previously safe displayed content and labels refresh failure instead of falsely presenting stale content as current;
- compact Impact / Effort / Confidence indicators with restrained semantic supporting tints;
- quick actions: `Apply`, `Why?`, `Later`, `Not relevant`;
- `Later` maps to the existing Skip decision semantics (`Not the right time`) and introduces no new backend state;
- Why Now, Evidence, assumptions, limitations, expiry and Knowledge Pack metadata remain in Opportunity Detail through progressive disclosure rather than competing on Today;
- concise loading, insufficient-context, no-focus and degraded states;
- missing-goal recovery explicitly distinguishes suggested starter goals from persisted owner goals;
- preserved accessibility labels, minimum touch targets, Dynamic Type-compatible layout and provider-neutral owner copy;
- preserved the four persistent tabs: Today / History / Goals / Profile;
- preserved cross-industry behavior with no restaurant-specific product logic or fabricated BI data.

- Today remains a decision screen, not a dashboard.
- One primary Best move and one primary Apply action.
- No filler recommendation and no weakening of VS-30 factual-evidence eligibility.
