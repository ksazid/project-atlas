# Atlas Product Glossary v1.0

This glossary is authoritative across product, design, engineering, analytics and support.

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Account | The authenticated identity belonging to a person using Atlas. |
| Action | A concrete step the owner can take in response to an Opportunity. |
| Applied | The owner chose to act, but completion is not yet confirmed. |
| Atlas Core | Industry-agnostic platform capabilities shared across Businesses and Knowledge Packs. |
| Business | The organization using Atlas and the primary tenant boundary. |
| Business Context | Current and historical facts used to reason about a Business. |
| Business Goal | An outcome the owner wants Atlas to prioritize. |
| Business Intelligence | Converting context, evidence, goals, memory and domain knowledge into useful understanding and action. |
| Business Memory | Structured relevant history used to improve future intelligence. |
| Business Owner | The primary customer responsible for the Business and its decisions. |
| Business Profile | Owner-facing identity, category, location, hours, channels, goals, timezone, currency and preferences. |
| Candidate Opportunity | A potential Opportunity before eligibility and prioritization. |
| Completed | The owner reports that the Action has been completed. |
| Confidence | Atlas’s assessment of evidence strength and contextual support; not a guarantee. |
| Context Fact | A normalized fact with source, freshness and confidence. |
| Decision Intelligence | Helping the owner choose and execute the strongest practical action. |
| Evidence | Facts or observations supporting an Opportunity. |
| Execution Asset | One prepared item within an Execution Kit. |
| Execution Kit | Prepared materials and instructions needed to complete an Action. |
| Expected Impact | A directional description of potential value, not a measured result. |
| Expired | The Opportunity is no longer timely or valid. |
| Generic Business Knowledge Pack | The first cross-industry pack, intentionally limited to broad business intelligence. |
| Growth Opportunity | A validated, explainable situation worth attention because action may improve a material outcome. |
| History | The chronological customer view of Opportunities, Actions, feedback, statuses and Outcomes. |
| Impact Level | Directional potential value: Low, Moderate or High. |
| Insight | A useful observation that may not itself be actionable. |
| Intelligence Run | One controlled cycle that loads context, applies packs, generates candidates, validates and selects. |
| Knowledge Pack | A versioned module containing domain terminology, rules, evidence requirements, templates and safeguards. |
| Limitation | A known weakness, missing fact or uncertainty affecting an Opportunity. |
| Measured Outcome | An Outcome supported by direct quantitative evidence. |
| Not Relevant | The owner says the Opportunity does not fit the Business or present situation. |
| Opportunity | Short form for Growth Opportunity when context is clear. |
| Opportunity Detail | The view explaining evidence, reason, confidence, impact, effort, assumptions and limitations. |
| Outcome | What happened after an Action; measured, owner-reported, estimated or unknown. |
| Owner-Confirmed Data | Business data explicitly reviewed and accepted by the owner. |
| Owner-Reported Outcome | An Outcome described by the owner without direct measurement. |
| Pilot Operator | An authorized internal user supporting pilot quality and assistance. |
| Platform Administrator | A highly restricted internal platform role. |
| Public Business Data | Lawfully obtained public information about a Business. |
| Reason | The human-readable explanation of why an Opportunity matters. |
| Rejected | The owner declines an Opportunity and records a reason where possible. |
| Signal | A raw input such as a goal, review, owner input, sales change or event. |
| Skipped | The owner defers or declines an Opportunity without declaring it fundamentally irrelevant. |
| Source Attribution | Metadata identifying where a Context Fact or Evidence item came from. |
| Today | The primary mobile destination. |
| Today’s Focus | The customer-facing presentation of the strongest current Growth Opportunity. |
| Unknown Outcome | Atlas lacks sufficient evidence to determine what happened. |
| Weekly Review | A narrative summary of Opportunities, Actions, Outcomes, feedback and learning. |
| Why Now | The timing or urgency explanation behind an Opportunity. |

## Naming rules

1. Use **Business**, not workspace, tenant, company or organization in customer-facing language unless legally required.
2. Use **Business Owner** or **owner** where the role matters.
3. Use **Today’s Focus**, not daily task, tip, alert or daily recommendation.
4. Use **Growth Opportunity** in specifications and **Opportunity** where unambiguous.
5. Use **Execution Kit**, not toolkit, content bundle or action pack.
6. Use **Knowledge Pack**, not industry module, brain, plugin, skill or vertical engine.
7. Use **Expected Impact** before action and **Outcome** after action.
8. Use **Confidence** only for evidence strength, never guaranteed success.
9. Use **Signal** only for raw inputs; Signals are not customer value by themselves.
10. Use **Business Intelligence** for the product category and **Decision Intelligence** for selecting and acting on the strongest practical action.
